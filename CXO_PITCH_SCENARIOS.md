Each CXO role has 3 typical problem scenarios. For each pitch, follow the 4-part structure:
1. **Problem & Cost** - What's broken and what it's costing them
2. **Solution & ROI** - How Camino fixes it and the financial return
3. **Low Risk Offer** - Easy way to get started
4. **Next Steps** - Clear action to move forward
