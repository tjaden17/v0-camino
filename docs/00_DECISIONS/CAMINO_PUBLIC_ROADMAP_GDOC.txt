🟢 LIVE — Available Now

These problems are solved. You can use these features today.


Problem 1: "I spend hours every week pulling reports from different tools"

The Pain:
You manually export CSVs from Zoho, HubSpot, or other tools every week. It's repetitive busywork that takes time away from actually running your business.

What We Built:
Upload your data once, and Camino instantly analyzes it. No more manual exports every week—just upload when you have new data, and we'll show you what matters.

Status: Live ✓


Problem 2: "I see numbers but don't know what they mean for my business"

The Pain:
Your tools show you raw metrics—"143 open tickets" or "$47K pipeline"—but they don't explain if that's good, bad, or what to do about it.

What We Built:
AI-powered interpretations that explain what each number means for YOUR role and goals. No more guessing—Camino tells you if a metric is healthy, concerning, or critical, and why.

Status: Live ✓


Problem 3: "My team wastes time in meetings trying to understand the same data"

The Pain:
Everyone interprets metrics differently. Your sales manager sees "pipeline growing" while you see "close rates dropping." Meetings turn into debates about what the numbers mean.

What We Built:
Role-based views so each person sees metrics that matter to them. Executives see high-level trends, managers see team performance, and everyone's looking at the same truth.

Status: Live ✓

Problem 4: "I have to manually re-upload data every month"

The Pain:
Once you've uploaded your data, you need to do it again next month. And the month after. Manual uploads become a recurring tax on your time.

What We're Building:
Direct API integration with Zoho, HubSpot, and other tools for automatic data sync. Set it once, and Camino automatically updates with your latest data—daily, weekly, or monthly.

Why This Matters:
Your insights stay current without any manual work. No more stale data or forgotten uploads.

Target: Goal 1 (March 2026)


Problem 5: "The data has errors and inconsistencies I have to clean manually"

The Pain:
Duplicate records, automated system tickets mixed with customer issues, test data polluting your metrics. You spend time cleaning data before you can trust it.

What We're Building:
Smart filtering that automatically removes noise—duplicates, automated tickets, test records. Camino learns what's signal and what's noise for your business.

Why This Matters:
Trustworthy metrics from day one. No manual cleanup required.

Target: Goal 1 (March 2026)


Problem 6: "I don't know if my metrics are getting better or worse"

The Pain:
Point-in-time numbers don't tell you the story. "143 tickets" means nothing without knowing if that's up, down, or stable compared to last week.

What We're Building:
Period-over-period comparisons built into every signal. See this week vs last week, this month vs last month, this quarter vs last quarter—automatically.

Why This Matters:
Understand trends at a glance. Know if things are improving, declining, or stable without building spreadsheets.

Target: Goal 1 (March 2026)

Problem 7: "I use multiple tools and can't see the full picture"

The Pain:
Deals in HubSpot, tickets in Zendesk, revenue in Stripe. You need to mentally connect the dots across tools to understand what's really happening.

What We're Exploring:
Cross-source signals that automatically connect related data. See how support tickets correlate with deal closures, or how customer health impacts revenue—without manual analysis.

Why This Matters:
Understand cause and effect across your entire business, not just within one tool.

Research Phase: Goals 2-3 (April-June 2026)


Problem 8: "I want custom metrics specific to my business"

The Pain:
Standard reports don't match how you measure success. You have unique KPIs that matter to your industry or business model.

What We're Exploring:
Flexible signal builder that lets you define custom metrics using your data. Track what matters to YOU, not just what's pre-built.

Why This Matters:
Every business measures success differently. Generic dashboards force you into someone else's framework.

Research Phase: Goals 2-3 (April-June 2026)


Problem 9: "My data changes constantly and insights get stale"

The Pain:
By the time you analyze data and make a decision, it's outdated. You're always looking at last week's or last month's picture.

What We're Exploring:
Daily or weekly automated updates so your insights are always current. No more analyzing stale data.

Why This Matters:
Make decisions based on what's happening now, not what happened two weeks ago.

Research Phase: Goals 2-3 (April-June 2026)

Problem 10: "I need to share insights with my board or investors"

The Pain:
You screenshot dashboards, paste into slides, and email manually. Updating your board deck is busywork.

Potential Solution:
Shareable reports with scheduled delivery. Generate a board-ready view and have it automatically emailed to stakeholders weekly or monthly.

Status: Evaluating demand


Problem 11: "I want to benchmark against similar companies"

The Pain:
You don't know if your metrics are good or bad compared to other businesses like yours. Is 8% churn high? Is 23% close rate competitive?

Potential Solution:
Anonymous peer benchmarks from our customer base (with your permission). See how your performance compares to similar-sized businesses in your industry.

Status: Evaluating demand

HOW WE PRIORITIZE

We build based on three factors:

1. Customer Impact — Does this solve a painful problem for multiple customers?
2. Technical Feasibility — Can we build this well with our current architecture?
3. Strategic Fit — Does this move us toward our long-term vision?

Your feedback directly influences what moves from Backlog → Researching → Building → Live.

WHAT WE WON'T BUILD

Some things intentionally aren't on our roadmap:

❌ Complex BI tools — We're focused on executives who need quick insights, not analysts who need pivot tables.

❌ Custom visualizations — Stock ticker format is proven for quick comprehension. We won't add chart builders or custom graphs.

❌ Workflow automation — We show you what matters; your other tools handle the actions.

Our focus: Make business data understandable for busy leaders in under 60 seconds.
