Available now
BUILDING	"I have to manually re-upload data every month"	You solved the problem once by uploading a CSV. Next month, you do it all over again. Manual, repetitive work that should be automated.	API integration with Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce. Connect once, data syncs automatically. Set it and forget it.	March 2026
BUILDING	"The data has errors and inconsistencies I have to clean manually"	Duplicate records, test accounts, automated tickets, bad formatting. You can't trust the numbers without hours of cleaning.	Smart filtering removes noise automatically. Learns what's signal vs noise in your data. One-click cleanup that gets smarter over time.	March 2026
BUILDING	"I don't know if my metrics are getting better or worse"	Point-in-time numbers with no trend context. "21 open tickets" — is that up or down? Improving or deteriorating?	Period-over-period comparisons (vs last week/month/quarter). Trend indicators show direction and velocity. Catch problems early.	March 2026

Flexible signal builder for your unique KPIs. Define custom formulas, thresholds, and interpretations that match your business.	Mid-Late 2026
RESEARCHING	"My data changes constantly and insights get stale"	By the time you analyze data, it's outdated. Business moves faster than your reporting cycle.	Daily/weekly automated updates. Fresh signals delivered on your schedule. Always working from current data.	Late 2026

WHAT WE WON'T BUILD (OPTIONAL SECOND TABLE)

Focus Area	What We Won't Do	Why
Visualization	Complex dashboards with 20+ charts, Tableau/Power BI features	We focus on "what does this mean?" not "how many ways can we show it?"
Target User	SQL query builders, data warehouse connectors, complex ETL pipelines	We serve small business operators, not data analysts. Keep it simple enough for a CEO to use without training.
Philosophy	Show everything, 80+ generic metrics	We'd rather have 8 perfect signals than 80 mediocre ones. We curate what matters.

KEY INFORMATION (TEXT SECTIONS)

How We Prioritize:
• Customer feedback drives priorities — If 5 customers need Problem 7 before Problem 6, we adapt
• We validate before building — Problems move from Backlog → Research → Building only after customer validation
• Transparency matters — We update this quarterly and explain why priorities shifted

Your Voice Matters:
Email feedback@camino.app to influence what we build next. We read every message and many features above came from customer conversations.

About Camino:
Camino helps small business executives understand their data without becoming data analysts. Upload your exports, get instant insights in a format designed for quick daily reviews.
