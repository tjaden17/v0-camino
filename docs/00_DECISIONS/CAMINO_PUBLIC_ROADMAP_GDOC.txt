✅ LIVE (Available Now)

Problem 1: "I spend hours every week pulling reports from different tools"

User Pain: Manually exporting CSVs from Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce, etc. Copy-pasting into spreadsheets. Formatting data. By the time you're done, the insights are stale.

Impact: 3-5 hours/week of manual work that could be spent on strategy.

Our Solution: Upload once, see signals immediately. Camino automatically maps your columns, calculates key metrics, and surfaces what matters. No more manual data wrangling.

Current Status: Works with any CSV/Excel export from major business tools.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem 2: "I see numbers but don't know what they mean for my business"

User Pain: Raw metrics without context. "Pipeline value is $2.3M" - is that good? Compared to what? What should I do about it?

Impact: Data paralysis. You have information but can't make decisions.

Our Solution: AI explains what each signal means for YOUR role and goals. "Pipeline is 15% below target and trending down. This impacts Q1 revenue forecasts."

Current Status: Every signal includes context-aware interpretation.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem 3: "My team wastes time in meetings trying to understand the same data"

User Pain: Sales sees pipeline, CS sees tickets, executives want both connected. Everyone interprets metrics differently. Meetings become data debates.

Impact: 2+ hours/week in alignment meetings that should be 15 minutes.

Our Solution: Role-based views. Executives see high-level signals, managers see their team's metrics, reps see their individual performance. Everyone looking at the same truth.

Current Status: Profiles for Executive, Sales Manager, CS Manager roles.

🔨 BUILDING (In Progress - Goal 1)

Problem 4: "I have to manually re-upload data every month"

User Pain: You solved the problem once by uploading a CSV. Next month, you do it all over again. Manual, repetitive work that should be automated.

Impact: The first upload saves time, but ongoing uploads waste it back.

Our Solution: API integration with Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce. Connect once, data syncs automatically. Set it and forget it.

Current Status: Building Zoho CRM + Zoho Desk integration for pilot customer.

Target: March 2026

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem 5: "The data has errors and inconsistencies I have to clean manually"

User Pain: Duplicate records, test accounts, automated tickets, bad formatting. You can't trust the numbers without hours of cleaning.

Impact: Either accept bad data or spend time fixing it manually.

Our Solution: Smart filtering removes noise automatically. Learns what's signal vs noise in your data. One-click cleanup that gets smarter over time.

Current Status: Building intelligent filters for Locumate's support tickets.

Target: March 2026

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem 6: "I don't know if my metrics are getting better or worse"

User Pain: Point-in-time numbers with no trend context. "21 open tickets" - is that up or down? Improving or deteriorating?

Impact: Can't spot problems until they're critical. Reactive instead of proactive.

Our Solution: Period-over-period comparisons (vs last week/month/quarter). Trend indicators show direction and velocity. Catch problems early.

Current Status: Designing trend visualization for signal cards.

Target: March 2026

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

🔍 RESEARCHING (Validating with Customers - Goals 2-3)

Problem 7: "I use multiple tools and can't see the full picture"

User Pain: Deals in HubSpot, tickets in Zendesk, revenue in Stripe. Each tool shows part of the story. You manually connect the dots in your head.

Impact: Miss patterns like "deals with slow response times churn faster."

Our Solution: Cross-source signals that connect the dots automatically. See how sales affects support, support affects retention, retention affects revenue.

Validation: Testing with customers who use 2+ data sources.

Timeline: Mid 2026

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem 8: "I want custom metrics specific to my business"

User Pain: Standard reports don't match how you measure success. Your KPIs are unique to your business model.

Impact: Forced to calculate manually or use generic metrics that don't matter.

Our Solution: Flexible signal builder for your unique KPIs. Define custom formulas, thresholds, and interpretations that match your business.

Validation: Gathering requirements from early customers.

Timeline: Mid-Late 2026

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem 9: "My data changes constantly and insights get stale"

User Pain: By the time you analyze data, it's outdated. Business moves faster than your reporting cycle.

Impact: Making decisions on yesterday's information.

Our Solution: Daily/weekly automated updates. Fresh signals delivered on your schedule. Always working from current data.

Validation: Determining optimal refresh frequencies for different roles.

Timeline: Late 2026

WHAT WE WON'T BUILD

We focus on interpretation, not visualization
- We won't build complex dashboards with 20+ charts
- We won't compete with Tableau or Power BI
- We focus on "what does this mean?" not "how many ways can we show it?"

We serve small business operators, not data analysts
- No SQL query builders
- No data warehouse connectors
- No complex ETL pipelines
- Keep it simple enough for a CEO to use without training

We're opinionated about quality over quantity
- We'd rather have 8 perfect signals than 80 mediocre ones
- We curate what matters, we don't show everything
