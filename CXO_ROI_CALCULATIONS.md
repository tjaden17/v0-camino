# Camino ROI Calculations by CXO Role

## How to Use This Document
- **Low Estimate**: Conservative scenario with minimal adoption and impact
- **High Estimate**: Optimistic scenario with strong adoption and full impact
- **Assumptions**: Based on industry benchmarks and typical mid-market tech companies ($10M-$100M ARR)
- **Customize**: Adjust baseline numbers to match your prospect's actual metrics

---

## 1. CEO - Strategic Misalignment Problem

### Problem Being Solved
Executives making decisions on outdated or conflicting data, leading to strategic misalignment and missed opportunities.

### Baseline Assumptions
- Company ARR: $25M
- Number of strategic decisions per quarter: 20
- Average revenue impact per strategic decision: $500K
- Current decision accuracy (based on incomplete data): 60%
- Decision accuracy with Camino (real-time aligned data): 75% (low) to 85% (high)

### ROI Calculation

**Low Estimate (75% accuracy):**
- Improved decisions per quarter: 20 × (75% - 60%) = 3 additional correct decisions
- Revenue impact: 3 × $500K = $1.5M per quarter = **$6M annually**
- Camino cost: $50K annually (10 exec users × $5K/year)
- **Net ROI: $5.95M (11,900% ROI)**

**High Estimate (85% accuracy):**
- Improved decisions per quarter: 20 × (85% - 60%) = 5 additional correct decisions
- Revenue impact: 5 × $500K = $2.5M per quarter = **$10M annually**
- Camino cost: $50K annually
- **Net ROI: $9.95M (19,900% ROI)**

### Key Assumptions
- Strategic decisions have measurable revenue impact (conservative $500K average)
- Improved data visibility increases decision quality by 15-25%
- Time savings from reduced context-switching not included in calculation

## How to Customize for Your Prospect

### Step 1: Gather Baseline Metrics
Ask during discovery:
- "What's your current [metric]?" (e.g., churn rate, forecast accuracy, CAC)
- "How much time does your team spend on [activity]?" (e.g., data gathering, ad-hoc reports)
- "How many [units] do you have?" (e.g., engineers, sales reps, customers)

### Step 2: Adjust Assumptions
- Use their actual numbers instead of baseline assumptions
- Be conservative with improvement percentages
- Focus on metrics they already track and care about

### Step 3: Calculate Custom ROI
- Use the formulas provided above
- Show your work (transparency builds trust)
- Present both low and high estimates

### Step 4: Validate with Prospect
- "Does this math make sense based on your business?"
- "Are these improvement percentages realistic for your team?"
- "What other impacts should we consider?"

## Common Objections & Responses

**"These ROI numbers seem too high"**
- "You're right to be skeptical. Let's cut these in half and it's still a 3-month payback."
- "What if we only achieve 50% of the low estimate? That's still [X ROI]."
- "These are based on time savings and decision improvements. Which part seems unrealistic?"

**"We need to see proof from similar companies"**
- "Fair ask. Here's what we've seen: [share case study or beta results]"
- "Let's do a 30-day pilot focused on [specific metric] so you can validate this yourself."
- "What would you need to see in a pilot to feel confident?"

**"Our situation is different"**
- "Absolutely. Let's customize this model with your actual numbers."
- "What metrics matter most to you? We'll focus the ROI there."
- "That's why we're starting with a pilot—to prove the ROI in your specific context."
