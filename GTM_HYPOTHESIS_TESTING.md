# GTM Hypothesis Testing Framework
## Hooks & Channels by Executive Segment

### Overview
This document outlines 3 high-conviction hooks (messages most likely to engage) for each of 3 target segments (CEO, CPO, CSO), across 3 optimal channels per segment. Use this for A/B testing your outbound campaigns.

---

## 1. CEO Segment

### Top 3 Channels
1. **LinkedIn (Executive Feed)**
2. **Industry Conferences & Events**
3. **Peer Executive Networks (YPO, EO, Pavilion)**

### Hook A: "The 3-Hour Board Deck Problem"
**Hypothesis:** CEOs are frustrated spending excessive time preparing board reports instead of running the company.

**Message:**
> "Stop spending 3+ hours before every board meeting hunting for metrics. Camino gives you a living dashboard that answers 'How's the business?' in 30 seconds. One CEO cut board prep from 4 hours to 15 minutes."

**Channel-Specific Tactics:**
- **LinkedIn:** Sponsored post with poll "How long do you spend prepping for board meetings?"
- **Conferences:** Speaking opportunity on "The Real Cost of Manual Reporting"
- **Peer Networks:** Case study share in Slack/Circle communities

**Success Metrics:** 
- Click-through rate >3%
- Demo booking rate >20%
- Message resonance score (survey: "This describes my problem" >60%)

---

### Hook B: "Revenue Blind Spots"
**Hypothesis:** CEOs worry they're missing critical signals about revenue health until it's too late.

**Message:**
> "Your CFO gives you last month's numbers. Your CRO gives you this quarter's pipeline. But what about the signals that predict next quarter's miss? Camino connects the dots between product engagement, sales velocity, and churn risk—before they hit your P&L."

**Channel-Specific Tactics:**
- **LinkedIn:** Video from CEO testimonial showing revenue recovery story
- **Conferences:** Booth demo: "Spot revenue risks 90 days early"
- **Peer Networks:** Host roundtable on "Leading Indicators That Actually Work"

**Success Metrics:**
- Video completion rate >45%
- Booth demo conversion >25%
- Roundtable attendance >15 CEOs

---

### Hook C: "The Alignment Tax"
**Hypothesis:** CEOs are bleeding cash on misaligned teams working toward different goals.

**Message:**
> "Misalignment costs $2.3M/year for a 100-person company. Your eng team optimizes for uptime while sales needs new features. Your product team ships what users request while your CFO needs margin improvement. Camino creates one shared scoreboard so everyone rows in the same direction."

**Channel-Specific Tactics:**
- **LinkedIn:** ROI calculator post "What's your alignment tax?"
- **Conferences:** Workshop on "Building a Unified Operating System"
- **Peer Networks:** Share "Alignment Tax Calculator" spreadsheet

**Success Metrics:**
- Calculator completion rate >40%
- Workshop sign-ups >30 attendees
- Spreadsheet downloads >100

**Hypothesis:** CSOs struggle to forecast accurately because they rely on rep intuition instead of data signals.

**Message:**
> "Your forecast is off by 20%+ every quarter because your reps say 'It feels like it'll close.' Camino analyzes buyer engagement patterns across product usage, meeting frequency, and content interaction to predict deal close probability with 85% accuracy. Stop guessing. Start knowing."

**Channel-Specific Tactics:**
- **LinkedIn:** Video case study: "How we improved forecast accuracy from 68% to 91%"
- **Sales Communities:** AMA in Revenue Collective on "Data-driven forecasting"
- **Conferences:** Demo station: "Predict which deals will close this quarter"

**Success Metrics:**
- Video views >10K
- AMA participants >100
- Demo conversions >35%

**Channel-Specific Tactics:**
- **LinkedIn:** Carousel: "7 hidden patterns of top-performing sales reps"
- **Sales Communities:** Case study post in Sales Hacker
- **Conferences:** Breakout session: "Scaling your top 10% across the team"

**Success Metrics:**
- Carousel shares >300
- Case study reads >2K
- Session attendance >50 CSOs

### Hook C: "The Deal Velocity Gap"

**Success Metrics:**

- Webinar registrations >200
- Live diagnostics completed >40

## Testing Framework

### Hypothesis Testing Process

**Week 1-2: Launch Phase**
- Deploy all 9 hooks across channels simultaneously
- Track engagement metrics (CTR, views, responses)
- Collect qualitative feedback (comments, DMs, survey responses)

**Week 3-4: Analysis Phase**
- Identify top 3 hooks by segment (based on engagement + demo bookings)
- Interview 5+ people who engaged to understand resonance
- Calculate cost per qualified lead (CPQL) for each hook

**Week 5-6: Iteration Phase**
- Double down on top-performing hooks
- A/B test variations of winning messages
- Kill bottom 3 performers

**Success Criteria by Hook:**
| Metric | Tier 1 (Keep) | Tier 2 (Test) | Tier 3 (Kill) |
|--------|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| Engagement Rate | >3% | 1.5-3% | <1.5% |
| Demo Booking Rate | >20% | 10-20% | <10% |
| Message Resonance | >60% | 40-60% | <40% |
| CPQL | <$200 | $200-400 | >$400 |
